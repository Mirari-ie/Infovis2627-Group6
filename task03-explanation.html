Gestalt principle How is it applied in the dashboard? Why?
Similarity Weekdays are represented in blue, while weekends are represented in orange. Using different colours helps viewers distinguish between the two categories and quickly identify which data belongs to each group.
Figure-Ground Relationship The blue and orange areas stand out against the white background. - This contrast makes the data the main focus of the visualization and helps viewers concentrate on the accident distribution throughout the day.
Symmetry The graph is divided into two sides around a central vertical axis, with weekdays on the left and weekends on the right. This balanced structure makes the comparison between the two categories easier and creates a clear visual organisation.
Continuity The horizontal gridlines guide the viewer's eyes across both distributions at the same hours of the day. They help viewers follow the graph smoothly and compare accident patterns between weekdays and weekends.
