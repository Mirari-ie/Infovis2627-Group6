Task 2 explanation

Our dashboard is destined to help an urban planning research group which is focused on accident prevention. This group is integrated by a multidisciplinary team of researchers, including engineers, mathematicians, physicists and social scientists who have an advanced knowledge in statistics and visualization. The dashboard is intended to be used by the researchers on their desktop computers and the data should be updated at least once a year but more frequently if any change proposed by the team has reccently been implemented in the city. The main goal of the dashboard is to provide a visualization of the data in a way that helps the reasearchers identify patterns in the data and, if possible, find a raltion between those patterns and accident causality.
